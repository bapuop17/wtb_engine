Estonia to Close Border with Russia After 'sabotage Attack' on Defense Firm

Published November 15, 2026 • Category: GEOPOLITICS • Region: Russia • Watch Directly on YouTube

1. BORDER SHUT DOWN

Estonia is shutting its border with Russia starting December. PM Karsten Michal says this stops hostile GRU activities.

2. GRU ARSON PLOT

Russian GRU agents tried to burn down Threod Systems. It's a drone factory in Viimsi near Tallinn.

3. 8 SUSPECTS CAUGHT

Estonian police detained eight people overnight for the plot. Moscow has not commented on these serious claims.
